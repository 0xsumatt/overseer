How this works

Price: one venue draws candles. Several venues draw close-price lines; the space between lines is the price difference between venues.

Spread is the ask minus the bid, in basis points of the midpoint.

Realised volatility covers the rolling window and is not annualised. Each point measures the preceding rolling window from completed 1-minute closes: 100 × the square root of the summed squared log returns.

A window with missing candles is incomplete and leaves a gap in the line. The legend shows each venue's value and its coverage, the share of the window's minutes with a close.
