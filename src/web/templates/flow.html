How this works

Taker imbalance is (taker buys − taker sells) ÷ volume for each venue minute: +100% means every aggressive trade bought, −100% means every one sold. Settlement times are marked on the line.

Large trades counts fills of $10K+ notional per minute; large imb. is the same imbalance measured on those fills only.

Venues affect charts and totals; thresholds filter only the detail table.
