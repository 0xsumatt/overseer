How this works

Positive funding means longs pay shorts; negative funding means shorts pay longs. Each venue settles on its own interval, so the table compares annualised rates by default.

Annualised rates assume the current rate holds for a year. Funding changes every period, so they are not a promised return.

Markets are sorted by the widest APR spread between venues, so dislocations surface first. Cell shading deepens with the size of the annualised rate; the strongest and weakest venue in each row are set in bold. Switching to rate per period keeps each venue's own interval and leaves the sort order unchanged.

Hover or focus a rate for liquidity and settlement details; select it to open that market on the venue. The table never scrolls sideways: when its columns get tight it shows one decimal, and on narrow screens each asset lists its venues in a grid with the spread beside the asset.

History lines are smoothed per venue with an EWMA (α = 0.35) unless raw is selected. The legend shows each venue's latest raw settlement and total open interest, or their values at the hovered time (UTC). Stacked bars show open interest by venue. Select a venue in the legend to hide or show it.
