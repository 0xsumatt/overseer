Basis

How this works

Basis is how far the perpetual price sits above or below spot: (perpetual − spot) ÷ spot × 100. Compare the same asset on both sides.

The line is basis % on the right axis; bars are the funding APR of the perpetual leg on the left axis. A long spot / short perpetual position collects positive funding.

Funding APR annualises settled rates; it is not a promised return.
