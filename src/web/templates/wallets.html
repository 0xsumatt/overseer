How this works

Net flow is cumulative buys − sells in base units from the start of the selected window, so it shows drift from the window-start position, not absolute inventory. Price is the Hyperliquid perpetual close.

Line colour follows each wallet's venue; two wallets on the same venue are told apart by line style.

Wallet share compares a wallet's 24h filled notional with its venue's 24h volume, taken from the daily venue-wide sweep. The fills feed is best-effort and may miss fills, so wallet share may be understated.

Fill history totals every recorded fill for each wallet across all assets; sizes are in base units.
